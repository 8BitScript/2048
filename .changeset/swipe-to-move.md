The status row says SWIPE TO MOVE on a phone or a tablet in the hands, where there are no arrows to press, and SWIPE OR ARROWS everywhere both work — a desktop, a touchscreen laptop, a tablet from the first arrow a plugged-in keyboard sends. `host.keyboard()` in lib/host/ is the question: `Input.KEYBOARD` on the eight machines (a fact, folded — every 6502 build is byte-identical, the 4K PET still 2876), and the web's new `input.keyboard()` on the page. The fixed web replicas, which said nothing at all on a touchscreen because SWIPE OR ARROWS might have been a lie there, now say the same as the fluid grid. Needs the 8bitscript release that carries `input.keyboard()`.
