Every screen but the PET's is a header row and one footer row, as the VIC-20 became. The C64, the Commander X16 and the web (the fluid page and its C64 and VIC-20 skins) no longer stack the move hint, the copyright and the version on separate rows under the board: the footer, one blank row below the board, holds the copyright until the first move and then the move hint (USE ARROWS; SWIPE, ARROW on the web) and the version. The score ends on the board's right edge on every one of them, which on the C64 also stops the header's raster wobble pushing its last digit off the screen. Measured: C64 5704 bytes of program from 5470, CX16 4657 from 4357, VIC-20 8K 3977 from 3874.
