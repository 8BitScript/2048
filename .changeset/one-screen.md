The game is dealt as the program starts. The header is the title — the copyright, the version, and on the C64 the raster wobble — and the board is the sixteen tiles. Measured against the previous build: the C64 5334 → 5076 bytes of program (99 → 97 of RAM), the 4K PET 2892 → 2826 (66 → 62), the German 4K PET 3072 → 3003, under the 3071-byte ceiling, and the unexpanded VIC-20 3431 → 3316 (84 → 81).
